Roadster Aero: Road vs Race

A roadster at 150 mph with the rear wing stowed and raised (simpleFoam, k-ω SST, rolling road, 2.2–3.6M cells). The wing turns 218 lbf of lift into 448 lbf of downforce for 232 lbf more drag. 3D results with a movable smoke probe.

OpenFOAM · external aero · automotive

Cooling Tower Plume Dispersion

A 492 ft natural-draft cooling tower driven by buoyancy alone (buoyantSimpleFoam, k-ω SST, 1.16M cells). Phase 1: 97 % of design air flow, energy balance closes. Plume in a crosswind next.

OpenFOAM · buoyant flow · environmental

NACA 0012 Airfoil Validation
