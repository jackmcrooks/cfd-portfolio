NASCAR Truck External Aero

A steady RANS model of a NASCAR truck (2022 Ford F-150 body), built from scratch in OpenFOAM and refined step by step: simple body → wheels → moving ground → mesh study.

What I learned

  • Start simple on the numerics. SIMPLEC with U relaxation 0.9 diverged by iteration ~15, starting in the 40 mm gap between the front tire and wheel well. Plain SIMPLE (p 0.3, U 0.7) was stable.
  • First order, then second. Upwind for the first 30 iterations, then linearUpwind on the live run, since OpenFOAM re-reads its settings while it runs.
  • potentialFoam initialization hurt. It gave unphysical velocities on this mesh. A uniform 26.82 m/s start worked better.
  • Flow features match expectations: stagnation on the nose, acceleration over the windshield–roof corner (~35–40 m/s), separation off the roof onto the bed, and a long wake behind the tailgate spoiler.

Next steps

  • Run to convergence (Cd varying < 1% over 200 iterations)
